## Executive Summary
This thesis examines the effectiveness of Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) initiatives 
implemented by Tullow Oil Ghana Ltd, focusing on their impact on sustainable local community 
development and Ghana's extractive sector.

## Key Focus Areas
- Financial and operational assessment of CSR allocations.
- Community development impact metrics.
- Governance and compliance within the Ghanaian extractive industry.
